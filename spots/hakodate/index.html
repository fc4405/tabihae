Hokkaido · 函館山の夜景

Hakodate Night View

One of Japan's most celebrated night views, glowing between two bays.

  • Best: May, June, September, October, December
  • Modern & urban
  • Scenic nature
  • Time: 2-3 hours
Plan a December route with this spotTours & tickets nearby (Klook)

Why go

Mt. Hakodate rises at the tip of Hokkaido's southern port city, and from its summit you look down on a narrow stretch of land with the sea on both sides. At dusk the lights come on and form a glittering shape often compared to a curved hourglass. The view is widely counted among Japan's most famous night views. Most visitors ride the ropeway up before sunset to watch the sky fade from gold to deep blue.

What to do

  • Ride the ropeway up Mt. Hakodate
  • Watch sunset turn to city lights
  • See the city between two bays
  • Warm up with local seafood afterwards

Getting there

From Hakodate Station, take a bus or a tram toward the foot of the mountain in roughly 15-30 minutes, then ride the ropeway to the summit. Hakodate is about 1.5 hours by flight from Tokyo, or by train and Shinkansen from Honshu.

When to visit

Clear, calm evenings give the best view, and arriving before sunset secures a good spot. Fog can hide the city, so check the weather and keep your plans flexible.

Photo tips

  • Arrive before sunset to capture the color shift
  • Rest your phone or camera on a railing for sharp night shots
  • Switch off flash and lower your screen brightness

Nearby

  • Motomachi and Hachiman-zaka slope
  • Red Brick Warehouses
  • Hakodate Morning Market

Good to know

It is much colder and windier at the summit than in town, so bring a warm layer even in summer.

The summit is crowded at dusk, so avoid blocking the view, skip flash, and dress warmly.
