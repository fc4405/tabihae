Hyogo · 姫路城

Himeji Castle

Japan's most perfect original castle, white as a heron in flight.

  • Best: January, February, March, April, October, November, December
  • Retro Japan
  • Time: 2-3 hours (add 1 hour for Koko-en garden)
Plan a November route with this spotTours & tickets nearby (Klook)

Why go

Himeji Castle in Hyogo Prefecture is the finest surviving example of Japanese castle architecture and one of Japan's first UNESCO World Heritage Sites. Its main keep dates from the early 17th century and is surrounded by a maze of walls, gates and turrets designed for defence. The brilliant white plaster exterior earned it the nickname Shirasagi-jo, the White Heron Castle. A major restoration finished in the mid-2010s left the keep looking bright and clean, and it rises above its stone walls like something from a painting.

What to do

  • See the white main keep from the wide plaza in front of the castle
  • Climb the narrow wooden stairs inside the keep to the top floor
  • Walk the winding path through gates and walls built for defence
  • Visit Koko-en, the Edo-style garden beside the castle

Getting there

From Shin-Osaka or Osaka, a JR Shinkansen or JR Special Rapid train reaches Himeji Station in roughly 30 to 60 minutes. The castle is a straight walk of about 15 minutes down the main avenue from the station.

When to visit

Early April for cherry blossoms and mid-November for autumn leaves are the prettiest times, but also the busiest. Arrive at opening on a weekday for the quietest visit.

Photo tips

  • Shoot the keep from the long plaza in front of Otemon Gate for the classic symmetrical view
  • Frame the castle through cherry blossoms in spring or maple leaves in autumn
  • Use the garden pond at Koko-en for a reflection shot with the keep behind

Nearby

  • Koko-en, a series of walled Edo-style gardens next to the castle
  • Mt Shosha and Engyo-ji temple, reached by ropeway
  • Himeji Station area for local specialities such as anago (conger eel) dishes

Good to know

You will remove your shoes inside the keep and climb steep stairs, so wear socks and slip-on shoes. Check the official site for opening hours, ticket information and any restricted areas before you go.
