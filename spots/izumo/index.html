Shimane · 出雲大社

Izumo Taisha

Ancient shrine of matchmaking and a rope as heavy as a truck.

  • Best: May, October, November
  • Retro Japan
  • Time: 1.5-2 hours
Plan a November route with this spot

Why go

Izumo Taisha in Shimane is one of Japan's oldest and most important shrines, dedicated to Okuninushi, the deity associated with building the land and with good connections between people. It is popular with visitors hoping for love and good relationships. The Kagura-den hall is famous for its enormous shimenawa rope, and the main sanctuary is built in the ancient taisha-zukuri style. Surrounded by tall trees, it feels quiet, spacious and deeply rooted in Japanese myth.

What to do

  • See the giant shimenawa rope at the Kagura-den hall
  • Walk the long pine-lined approach to the shrine
  • Pray for good connections at the main sanctuary
  • Browse Shinmon-dori street for local souvenirs and food

Getting there

From Izumoshi Station take a local bus or the Ichibata Electric Railway to the shrine area, about 20-30 minutes. Izumoshi can be reached from Okayama on the Yakumo limited express or by air to Izumo Enmusubi Airport.

When to visit

Visit on a weekday morning for a calm atmosphere. Autumn is especially atmospheric, and the old tradition of gods gathering here is tied to the tenth month of the old calendar.

Photo tips

  • Stand back and tilt up to fit the full width of the shimenawa
  • Frame the pine-lined approach with the torii at its start
  • Use wide angles inside the grounds to capture the tall trees

Nearby

  • Inasa Beach and its sunset views
  • Hinomisaki Lighthouse on the Sea of Japan coast
  • Matsue Castle and Lake Shinji

Good to know

Remember the four claps, and avoid walking in the center of the path, which is traditionally left for the deity.

Here the prayer is two bows, four claps, then one bow, which differs from the usual two claps at most shrines.
