Aichi · ジブリパーク

Ghibli Park

Step inside Studio Ghibli films in a real Aichi forest

  • Best: All year
  • Anime & pop culture
  • Time: 5-8 hours (a full day)
Plan a November route with this spotTours & tickets nearby (Klook)

Why go

Ghibli Park opened in 2022 inside the Aichi Expo 2005 Commemorative Park in Nagakute, east of Nagoya. Rather than a ride-based theme park, it recreates settings from Studio Ghibli films within the existing woodland, with areas such as Hill of Youth, Dondoko Forest, Mononoke Village and Valley of Witches. Its handcrafted buildings, including a giant warehouse of shops and exhibits and a house from My Neighbor Totoro, feel remarkably true to the films. It is calm, walkable and ideal for fans who want to see the scenery up close.

What to do

  • The elevator tower and retro buildings of Hill of Youth
  • Satsuki and Mei's House in Dondoko Forest
  • The large Grand Warehouse with shops and indoor exhibits
  • Howl's Castle and the storybook streets of Valley of Witches

Getting there

From Nagoya Station take the Higashiyama subway line to Fujigaoka (about 30 minutes), then the Linimo maglev line to Ai-Chikyuhaku-Kinen-Koen (Ghibli Park) Station, a few minutes' walk from the park entrance.

When to visit

Weekdays outside school holidays are far calmer, and spring greenery and autumn leaves suit the forest setting. Mornings are the best time to explore before the afternoon crowds build.

Photo tips

  • Shoot the elevator tower from below with the retro buildings to capture the film mood
  • Visit Satsuki and Mei's House in the morning for soft light through the trees
  • Use small details such as signs, windows and gardens to tell the story beyond the big landmarks

Nearby

  • Aichi Expo 2005 Commemorative Park (Moricoro Park) itself
  • Toyota Automobile Museum in Nagakute
  • Osu Shopping Street in Nagoya

Good to know

Entry normally requires a date-specified ticket bought in advance, and popular dates and attractions can sell out, so book through the official site well ahead. Some areas may need separate timed tickets. Wear comfortable walking shoes for a large site.
