Osaka · ユニバーサル・スタジオ・ジャパン

Universal Studios Japan

Step into Mario's world and a Hogwarts castle in one day

  • Best: All year
  • Anime & pop culture
  • Modern & urban
  • Time: A full day, about 8-10 hours; allow longer if you want most rides and both major lands
Plan a November route with this spotTours & tickets nearby (Klook)

Why go

Universal Studios Japan is a large theme park on Osaka's waterfront that opened in 2001. It combines Hollywood-style rides and shows with themed lands such as the Wizarding World of Harry Potter and Super Nintendo World. Super Nintendo World, opened in 2021, lets you walk through a real-life Mushroom Kingdom, with interactive Power-Up Band games. Add festive seasonal events and you get a day that feels like stepping inside a movie or game.

What to do

  • Walk into Super Nintendo World and ride Mario Kart: Koopa's Challenge
  • Explore Hogsmeade village and Hogwarts castle in the Wizarding World of Harry Potter
  • Try butterbeer, a signature drink and treat in the Wizarding World
  • Catch seasonal parades and events, from Halloween to winter illuminations

Getting there

From Osaka Station, take the JR Loop Line to Nishikujo, then transfer to the JR Yumesaki Line to Universal City Station, about 15 minutes in total. The park entrance is a short walk from the station.

When to visit

Weekdays outside school holidays are the least crowded, while weekends, Golden Week and summer are very busy. Arrive before opening and check the official app for wait times and any timed-entry requirements for Super Nintendo World.

Photo tips

  • Take the classic photo in front of the Hogwarts castle, ideally early when crowds are thin
  • In Super Nintendo World, frame the green pipes, the Mario statue and Bowser's castle together
  • At dusk, shoot the glowing castle and the park's lights for a dramatic evening look

Nearby

  • Universal CityWalk Osaka, right outside the gates, with restaurants and shops
  • Osaka Aquarium Kaiyukan and the Tempozan Ferris wheel, a short ride away
  • Dotonbori in central Osaka for street food and neon lights after the park

Good to know

Buy your ticket in advance online, since popular dates can sell out. Bring a portable charger for the park app and check the official site for ticket types, Express Pass options and current policies.
