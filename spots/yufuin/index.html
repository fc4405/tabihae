Yufuin & Lake Kinrinko

Misty lake mornings and cafe-hopping in a hot-spring village.

  • Best: January, April, October, November, December
  • Onsen towns
  • Photogenic cafes
  • Scenic nature
  • Time: Half day to overnight

Why go

Yufuin is a relaxed hot-spring town in Oita, set beneath the twin-peaked Mt. Yufu. Its small Lake Kinrinko is known for morning mist that rises on cool days, drawing photographers at dawn. The main street, lined with boutiques, sweets shops, galleries and cafes, is a favorite for leisurely strolls. Many visitors stay at a ryokan with its own hot spring to complete the quiet, countryside mood.

What to do

  • See morning mist at Lake Kinrinko
  • Stroll the boutique and sweets street
  • Relax in a ryokan hot spring
  • Admire twin-peaked Mt. Yufu

Getting there

From Hakata Station in Fukuoka, take the JR limited express to Yufuin Station in about 2 hours, or a direct highway bus of similar length. The lake is a walk of around 20-30 minutes from the station.

When to visit

Cool autumn and winter mornings give the best lake mist, and autumn leaves add color. Visit early on a weekday for fewer crowds.

Photo tips

  • Arrive at dawn for mist rising off the lake
  • Frame Mt. Yufu behind the town on clear days
  • Shoot cafe interiors and sweets near window light

Nearby

  • Yunotsubo Kaido shopping street
  • Yufuin Floral Village
  • Beppu hot spring town

Good to know

Stay overnight if you can, since day-trippers fill the main street while mornings and evenings are calm.

Wash thoroughly before entering hot springs, and enjoy street snacks at the shop rather than while walking.
