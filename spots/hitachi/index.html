Ibaraki · 国営ひたち海浜公園

Hitachi Seaside Park

Hills of baby-blue flowers that melt into the sky.

  • Best: April, May, October
  • Scenic nature
  • Time: 3-4 hours
Plan a April route with this spot

Why go

Hitachi Seaside Park is a huge national park on the Ibaraki coast, famous for its seasonal flower hills. In spring, Miharashi Hill is covered in blue nemophila that seems to merge with the sky, while in autumn the round kochia bushes turn a vivid red. The park also has a small amusement area with a Ferris wheel, wide cycling paths and sea breezes. It is one of the most iconic flower landscapes in Japan and a favorite of photographers.

What to do

  • Blue nemophila carpet on Miharashi Hill in spring
  • Round red kochia bushes in autumn
  • Ride the Ferris wheel for a view over the flowers and sea
  • Rent a bicycle to cover the huge grounds

Getting there

From Tokyo, take the JR Joban Line from Ueno or Shinagawa to Katsuta Station, then a local bus to the park. The whole trip takes roughly two hours.

When to visit

Nemophila peaks from mid-April to early May and kochia turns red in October. Weekdays and early mornings are far less crowded during flower season.

Photo tips

  • Shoot low and wide so the blue flowers meet the sky
  • Use a longer lens to compress the hill into layers of color
  • Aim for morning light on clear days for the richest blue

Nearby

  • Nakaminato Fish Market for fresh seafood
  • Kairakuen garden in Mito on the way back
  • Oarai coast, a seaside town in Ibaraki

Good to know

The grounds are huge and mostly open, so bring sun protection and comfortable shoes, and expect long bus queues at peak bloom.

Stay on the paths and never step into the flower beds, however good the photo might be.
