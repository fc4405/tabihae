Zao Fox Village

Walk among free-roaming foxes in a snowy Tohoku village.

  • Best: January, February, March, December
  • Scenic nature
  • Time: 1.5-2 hours
Plan a December route with this spotTours & tickets nearby (Klook)

Why go

Zao Fox Village is an animal park in Shiroishi, Miyagi Prefecture, in the foothills of the Zao mountains, where several kinds of foxes live. Red foxes, silver foxes and others wander across open enclosures, many curled up asleep or trotting right past visitors. In winter the whole place is covered in snow, and the orange and silver coats against the white make charming photos. Feeding and holding opportunities are limited to specific times and rules, so follow staff guidance.

What to do

  • Red and silver foxes roaming free in open enclosures
  • Sleeping foxes curled up like fluffy balls
  • Feeding and holding opportunities under staff supervision
  • Snowy winter scenery with foxes in the white

Getting there

Take the Tohoku Shinkansen from Sendai to Shiroikoshi-Zao Station (about 20 minutes), then a taxi or local transport; services are limited, so check the official site.

When to visit

Winter, roughly December to February, gives snowy photos, and foxes are often livelier on cool mornings. Weekdays are quieter than weekends.

Photo tips

  • Crouch to fox eye level for intimate portraits.
  • Shoot against clean snow so orange and silver coats pop.
  • Use a fast shutter speed for foxes in motion and be patient for sleeping poses.

Nearby

  • Shiroishi Castle, a rebuilt castle in the old castle town
  • Zao Onsen, a hot spring resort and ski area
  • Zao Okama crater lake, with changing colors

Good to know

Dress warmly in winter and follow the rules: never chase or grab the foxes, keep fingers away from their mouths, and wash your hands afterward.
