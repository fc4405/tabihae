Hirosaki Castle Park

A castle moat carpeted in pink petals, deep in northern Tohoku.

  • Best: April, May, October, November
  • Retro Japan
  • Scenic nature
  • Time: 2-3 hours (half a day in peak blossom season)
Plan a November route with this spotTours & tickets nearby (Klook)

Why go

Hirosaki Castle Park in Aomori Prefecture surrounds one of the few original castle keeps left in Japan, built in the early 17th century. Around 2,600 cherry trees grow within the grounds, and in late April they bloom along the old moats and earthworks. When the petals fall they cover the moat surface in a pink carpet that is one of the park's most famous sights. The city also holds a winter snow lantern festival, so the castle looks beautiful in other seasons too.

What to do

  • See the moat covered in floating pink petals after the blooms fall
  • Walk under cherry trees around the castle grounds
  • Look up at the original castle keep and its stone walls
  • Visit during the winter snow lantern festival for a different mood

Getting there

From Tokyo, take the Shinkansen to Shin-Aomori, then the JR Ou Line to Hirosaki Station (about 40 minutes). From the station, take a local bus or walk about 30 minutes to the park.

When to visit

Late April to early May is the cherry blossom season and the peak for the petal-covered moat. Go early in the morning or on a weekday to avoid the biggest crowds.

Photo tips

  • Shoot the pink carpet on the outer moat from the bridge or the edge for a flat layered pattern
  • Frame the castle keep through blossoming branches
  • Visit at dawn for fewer people and soft reflections on the water

Nearby

  • Fujita Memorial Garden, a Japanese-style garden with a retro cafe
  • Saisho-in Five-Storied Pagoda, a lovely temple pagoda in the nearby woods
  • Hirosaki Apple Park, celebrating the city's famous apples

Good to know

Hotels in Hirosaki fill up very early for cherry blossom season, so book months ahead. Dress warmly, because evenings can still be chilly in late April. Check whether the castle keep itself is open, as it may be under maintenance work.
