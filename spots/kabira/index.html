Ishigaki · 川平湾

Kabira Bay

A sheltered bay where the sea shifts through every shade of blue.

  • Best: April, May, June, July, August, September, October
  • Scenic nature
  • Time: 1-1.5 hours, plus the boat ride
Plan a April route with this spotTours & tickets nearby (Klook)

Why go

Kabira Bay on the north coast of Ishigaki Island is a calm, sheltered inlet dotted with small green islets. Its water changes from pale aqua to deep sapphire depending on depth, tide and sunlight, and it is designated a Place of Scenic Beauty. Because of strong currents and pearl cultivation, swimming is not allowed, but glass-bottom boats let you look down at coral and tropical fish. The white sand and the green islets make every viewpoint feel like a postcard.

What to do

  • A glass-bottom boat ride over coral and tropical fish
  • The panorama of green islets and layered blue water from the viewpoint
  • Fine white sand along the shore, ideal for a relaxed stroll
  • Black pearl shops and local snacks near the bay

Getting there

From the Ishigaki Port Passenger Terminal or Ishigaki Airport, take a local bus (about 40-50 minutes) or a taxi or rental car to Kabira Bay. A rental car makes it easy to combine with other north-coast spots.

When to visit

Visit around midday on a sunny day, when the sun is high and the colours are most vivid. Avoid typhoon season and rough weather, and note that the bay is quieter on weekday mornings.

Photo tips

  • Shoot from the elevated viewpoint above the bay to capture the islets and colour gradient
  • Use a polarizing filter and shoot with the sun behind you to deepen the blues
  • Take photos from the glass-bottom boat looking down for coral and fish close-ups

Nearby

  • Yonehara Beach, popular for snorkelling
  • Tamatorizaki Observatory, a cliff-top view over the sea
  • Hirakubozaki lighthouse area at the northern tip of the island

Good to know

Swimming in the bay is prohibited, so bring a swimsuit only for other beaches. Glass-bottom boat operations depend on weather and tide, so check availability on the day.
