Toyama · 立山黒部アルペンルート

Tateyama Kurobe Alpine Route

Cross the Japan Alps through towering snow walls and a giant dam.

  • Best: April, May, June, October
  • Scenic nature
  • Time: A full day (about 6-9 hours end to end)
Plan a April route with this spot

Why go

The Tateyama Kurobe Alpine Route links Toyama and Nagano across the Northern Alps using a chain of cable cars, buses, a ropeway and a trolley bus. The most famous sight is the Yuki-no-Otani, a road cut between walls of snow that can rise very high in spring. Later in the season the route is known for Kurobe Dam, one of Japan's tallest dams, and for brilliant autumn colour. The route usually closes for winter, so check the season before you plan.

What to do

  • Walking between the snow walls of Yuki-no-Otani
  • Kurobe Dam and its sightseeing viewpoints
  • Mountain scenery from the cable car and ropeway
  • Autumn colour around the high plateau

Getting there

From Toyama Station, take the Toyama Chiho Railway to Tateyama Station and begin the route there. From Nagano, buses run to the Ogizawa side to cross in the other direction.

When to visit

Mid-April to June for the snow walls, and October for autumn colour. Start early because the mountain weather often clouds over by afternoon.

Photo tips

  • Stand at the base of the snow wall with a person in frame to show its scale
  • Photograph Kurobe Dam from the viewpoints near the top of the dam wall
  • Shoot from the cable car window on clear mornings for layered mountain views

Nearby

  • Toyama city and Toyama Glass Art Museum
  • Unazuki Onsen in Kurobe Gorge
  • Omachi or Matsumoto on the Nagano side

Good to know

It is cold at the top even in summer, so bring layers and sturdy shoes, and check the operating dates and any weather disruptions in advance.

Stay on marked paths, never pick or step on alpine plants, and carry all your rubbish back down.
