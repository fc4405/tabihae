Nakano Broadway

A retro mall where otaku treasures stack floor to ceiling.

  • Best: All year
  • Anime & pop culture
  • Time: 2-3 hours
Plan a November route with this spotTours & tickets nearby (Klook)

Why go

Nakano Broadway is a compact shopping complex that opened in the 1960s as a stylish residential and retail building. Over the decades it turned into one of Tokyo's best-known hubs of manga, anime and collectibles, home to many Mandarake shops. Narrow corridors are crammed with vintage toys, cels, trading cards and rare figures, each little shop run by a specialist. The slightly worn, retro atmosphere is part of the appeal.

What to do

  • Browse the many Mandarake shops for manga and collectibles
  • Hunt vintage toys, cels and rare figures on the upper floors
  • Try capsule-toy machines and display-case boutiques
  • Grab a snack or ice cream in the basement food area

Getting there

From Shinjuku, ride the JR Chuo Line rapid service to Nakano Station in about 5 minutes, then walk through the Sun Mall arcade from the north exit to the entrance. The Tozai Line also stops at Nakano.

When to visit

Weekday afternoons are calmer and easier for browsing the narrow aisles. Weekends are busier, and shops generally open around midday, so check the official site or each shop for hours.

Photo tips

  • Shoot the long, narrow corridors for a sense of depth and the density of goods
  • Ask before photographing inside shops, as many small stores restrict it
  • Capture the Sun Mall arcade approach and the building exterior in daylight

Nearby

  • Nakano Sun Mall, the covered shopping street leading in
  • Nakano Central Park, a green space with cafes
  • Koenji, one stop west, famous for vintage clothing

Good to know

Many small shops are cash-friendly and some limit photos or bag size, so bring some cash and travel light.
