Shimokitazawa

Tokyo's thrift-and-coffee maze for wandering without a plan.

  • Best: All year
  • Photogenic cafes
  • Retro Japan
  • Time: 2-3 hours
Plan a November route with this spotTours & tickets nearby (Klook)

Why go

Shimokitazawa, often called Shimokita, is a laid-back neighbourhood in Setagaya known for vintage clothing, record shops, small theatres and live music venues. Its narrow, mostly pedestrian lanes grew around the station junction, and the area has long been a home for young artists and musicians. Today it also has indie coffee bars, curry restaurants and quirky boutiques tucked into every corner. It is a place to wander without a plan.

What to do

  • Hunt for vintage clothing in the many second-hand shops
  • Browse record stores and small theatres
  • Sip coffee at an indie cafe tucked down a side street
  • Try a bowl of Japanese curry or soup curry

Getting there

From Shinjuku, take the Odakyu Line to Shimokitazawa Station in about 10 minutes, or from Shibuya take the Keio Inokashira Line (about 5 minutes). The shopping lanes start right outside the station.

When to visit

Afternoon on a weekday is relaxed and many shops are open. Weekend afternoons are livelier, while evenings suit live music and bars.

Photo tips

  • Capture the colourful signboards and bikes lining the narrow lanes
  • Shoot cafe interiors from the doorway with permission
  • Include the station area and its redeveloped passage for contrast with the old streets

Nearby

  • Kichijoji and Inokashira Park, on the same Inokashira Line
  • Gotokuji Temple, famous for its lucky cat figurines
  • Tomigaya and Yoyogi-Uehara for quiet cafe-hopping

Good to know

Lanes are narrow and shared with bikes and delivery carts, so keep to one side and watch for passing vehicles. Many shops open late morning or later.
