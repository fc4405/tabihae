Miyagi · 松島

Matsushima Bay

Pine-clad islands in one of Japan's three great views.

  • Best: January, February, April, May, October, November, December
  • Scenic nature
  • Retro Japan
  • Time: 3-4 hours

Why go

Matsushima is a bay in Miyagi dotted with hundreds of small pine-covered islands, and is ranked among Japan's three most scenic views alongside Miyajima and Amanohashidate. Poets such as Matsuo Basho have long been linked to its beauty. You can see the islands from sightseeing boats, walk to the small Godaido temple hall on the shore, and cross red bridges, all with the calm sea as a backdrop.

What to do

  • Take a sightseeing cruise among the islands
  • Visit Godaido hall by the sea
  • Cross the red Fukuurajima bridge
  • Try fresh oysters in season

Getting there

From Sendai take the JR Senseki Line to Matsushima-Kaigan Station, about 40 minutes; the harbour and Godaido are a short walk away.

When to visit

Autumn colours and clear winter days are lovely, and early morning or sunset gives the softest light on the bay.

Photo tips

  • Climb a viewpoint above the bay for a wide shot of the islands.
  • Frame pines in the foreground against the water.
  • Shoot Godaido and the red bridges at golden hour.

Nearby

  • Zuiganji temple
  • Entsuin temple
  • Sendai city

Good to know

The bay area gets windy and cold in winter, so dress warmly, and be aware that boat services depend on the weather.

Temples and shrines here are active places of worship, so be quiet and respectful and follow posted photography rules.
