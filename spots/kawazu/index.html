Shizuoka · 河津桜（伊豆）

Kawazu Cherry Blossoms, Izu

Pink sakura by the river while winter is still here

  • Best: February, March
  • Scenic nature
  • Onsen towns
  • Time: 2-3 hours (a full day with the train trip)
Plan a February route with this spot

Why go

Kawazu is a small town on the east coast of the Izu Peninsula, famous for its early-blooming cherry trees that flower from February into early March. The deep pink blossoms last longer than most cherry varieties, and many trees line the Kawazu River. Yellow rapeseed flowers often bloom beneath them, creating a vivid pink and yellow scene. A seasonal festival brings food stalls and lively crowds, and the area's hot springs make a warm finish to a chilly day.

What to do

  • Stroll under blossoms along the Kawazu River
  • See pink sakura above yellow rapeseed flowers
  • Browse festival food stalls in season
  • Soak in a local hot spring afterward

Getting there

From Tokyo, take a limited express such as the Odoriko to Kawazu Station on the Izu Kyuko line, about 2.5 hours. The river and blossoms are a short walk from the station.

When to visit

Blossoms usually peak from mid-February to early March, depending on the year. Visit on a weekday morning to avoid the festival crowds.

Photo tips

  • Frame pink blossoms against yellow rapeseed flowers
  • Shoot down the river so the tree line leads the eye
  • Use a low angle with blue sky behind the blossoms

Nearby

  • Kawazu Nanadaru waterfalls
  • Shimoda port town
  • Izu coast hot spring inns

Good to know

Bloom timing changes each year, so check the current blossom status before you go. Bring a warm layer, as it is still cold in February.

Do not touch or shake the branches and do not step into the rapeseed flower beds along the river.
