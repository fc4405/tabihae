Okinawa · 万座毛

Cape Manzamo

A cliff shaped like an elephant's trunk, glowing over the East China Sea.

  • Best: All year
  • Scenic nature
  • Time: 30-60 minutes
Plan a November route with this spotTours & tickets nearby (Klook)

Why go

Cape Manzamo is a rocky headland on the west coast of Okinawa's main island, in Onna Village. Its best-known feature is a limestone cliff eroded by waves into a shape that looks like an elephant's trunk. The name is often explained by a story that a Ryukyu king admired the broad grassy plateau on top and said it could seat ten thousand people. The cliff drops steeply into clear blue water, and the sunset over the East China Sea is the classic reason to visit.

What to do

  • See the elephant-trunk limestone cliff from the viewing path
  • Walk across the grassy plateau on top of the cape
  • Watch the sunset over the East China Sea
  • Look back at the coastline toward Mt Onna and Nabi beach

Getting there

From Naha, it is roughly 1 to 1.5 hours by car along the west coast, and a rental car is by far the easiest way. Express buses from Naha toward the Onna area also run; check current routes and timetables.

When to visit

Late afternoon to sunset gives the best light and colours. Avoid typhoon season weather, and bring sun protection for the exposed, shadeless walk.

Photo tips

  • Shoot the elephant-trunk cliff from the viewing path with the sea behind it
  • Come near sunset for warm light on the rock and glowing sky
  • Use a wide-angle view to include the coastline and Nabi beach

Nearby

  • Cape Maeda and its famous Blue Cave snorkelling area in Onna
  • Okinawa Churaumi Aquarium in Motobu, a drive to the north
  • Cape Zanpa and its lighthouse to the south in Yomitan

Good to know

Stay on the marked paths and behind the fences, since the cliff edge is steep and the wind can be strong. Parking is limited at sunset, so arrive a little early.
