Naha, Okinawa · 国際通り・牧志公設市場

Naha Kokusai-dori & Makishi Market

Eat, shop and wander Okinawa's busiest street from market to night.

  • Best: March, April, May, October, November
  • Modern & urban
  • Photogenic cafes
  • Time: 2-3 hours
Plan a November route with this spotTours & tickets nearby (Klook)

Why go

Kokusai-dori is Naha's main street, a long strip of souvenir shops, restaurants and eateries that was rebuilt after the war and is often called the Miracle Mile. Just off it, the covered arcades lead to Makishi Public Market, where you can see Okinawan fish, pork and tropical produce. Many market stalls will cook seafood you buy, giving first-timers an easy taste of local food. By night the street glows with signs and live music from small bars.

What to do

  • Souvenir shopping along Kokusai-dori
  • Fresh fish and tropical produce at Makishi Market
  • Okinawan street snacks and sweets
  • Evening live music in small bars

Getting there

From Naha Airport, take the Yui Rail monorail to Makishi or Kencho-mae Station, which are at the street's two ends. The ride takes about 15 minutes.

When to visit

Spring and autumn are comfortable for walking. Visit the market in the morning or at lunch, then return in the evening for the glowing street.

Photo tips

  • Shoot the main street at dusk when the shop signs light up
  • Capture colourful fish and produce at the market, asking vendors first
  • Look for shisa lion statues at shop doors as a fun local detail

Nearby

  • Shuri Castle area
  • Tsuboya Pottery Street
  • Heiwa-dori arcade shopping street

Good to know

Many small shops and stalls prefer cash, so carry some yen, and come hungry to try a few small dishes rather than one big meal.

Ask before photographing market vendors or their stalls, and avoid eating while walking in crowded arcades.
