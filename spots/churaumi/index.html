Churaumi Aquarium

Whale sharks and manta rays glide past a giant window onto the sea.

  • Best: All year
  • Scenic nature
  • Time: 2-3 hours for the aquarium; half a day with the park and beach
Plan a November route with this spotTours & tickets nearby (Klook)

Why go

Churaumi Aquarium sits in Ocean Expo Park on the northwest coast of Okinawa's main island and is one of Japan's most visited attractions. Its centerpiece is the Kuroshio Sea tank, behind one of the largest acrylic windows ever built, where whale sharks and manta rays swim with schools of fish. The aquarium focuses on the marine life of the Okinawan seas, from coral reefs to the deep. Sitting in front of the tank in the dim blue light is the moment most visitors remember.

What to do

  • Sit before the Kuroshio Sea tank and watch whale sharks and manta rays
  • Walk through the coral reef and tropical fish exhibits
  • See the free dolphin show at the Okichan Theater outside
  • Stroll the surrounding Ocean Expo Park and its beach

Getting there

From Naha, buses to Churaumi Aquarium take roughly 2 hours, or about 1.5-2 hours by rental car on the expressway and local roads. A rental car makes it easy to add stops along the north coast.

When to visit

Arrive near opening time or in the late afternoon to avoid the midday crowds. Weekdays are calmer, and rainy days are a good fit since most of the visit is indoors. Check the official site for feeding and show times.

Photo tips

  • Stand close to the acrylic window and avoid flash, as reflections spoil the shot
  • Raise ISO or use night mode since the tank room is dim, and wait for a whale shark to pass
  • For the building, shoot the exterior with the sea and sky behind it in the morning light

Nearby

  • Bise Fukugi Tree Road, a shaded village lane
  • Nakijin Castle ruins, a UNESCO World Heritage site
  • Kouri Island and its bridge over clear water

Good to know

Go straight to the Kuroshio Sea tank first, before it fills up, then work through the rest. Buy tickets in advance online if available, and check the official site for current hours and fees.
