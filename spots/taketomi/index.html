Ishigaki · 竹富島

Taketomi Island

Red roofs, white sand lanes and island time.

  • Best: April, May, June, July, October
  • Scenic nature
  • Retro Japan
  • Time: 3-5 hours (half to full day)

Why go

Taketomi is a small island in the Yaeyama Islands, reached by a short ferry ride from Ishigaki. Its village preserves traditional Ryukyuan scenery, with red-tile roofs, coral stone walls, shisa guardians and white-sand lanes. Visitors explore by walking, cycling or in a water buffalo cart, then relax on gentle beaches with clear blue water. Its slow pace and picture-perfect streets make it a favourite escape in Okinawa.

What to do

  • Walk the red-tile village with coral stone walls
  • Ride a water buffalo cart
  • Watch the sunset at Nishi Pier
  • Relax on Kondoi Beach's clear shallows

Getting there

From Ishigaki Port Ferry Terminal take a high-speed ferry to Taketomi, about 10 to 15 minutes; Ishigaki is reached by flight from Naha or other cities.

When to visit

Spring to early summer and early autumn have the best beach weather; avoid typhoon periods and the midday heat.

Photo tips

  • Shoot the white-sand lanes with red roofs under a blue sky.
  • Frame a shisa on a rooftop or gate for a classic Okinawa shot.
  • Capture sunset colours from the west pier.

Nearby

  • Ishigaki Yaima Village
  • Kabira Bay
  • Iriomote Island

Good to know

Keep an eye on the last return ferry, which leaves in the late afternoon, and bring sun protection and cash for rentals.

The village is home to residents, so stay on the paths, keep quiet and do not take sand, shells or coral away.
