Nara · 東大寺

Todai-ji Great Buddha

Stand before Japan's giant Buddha in a record-size wooden hall.

  • Best: March, April, October, November
  • Retro Japan
  • Scenic nature
  • Time: 1.5-2 hours
Plan a November route with this spotTours & tickets nearby (Klook)

Why go

Todai-ji was founded in the 8th century under Emperor Shomu and became the head temple of Buddhist temples across the country. Its Great Buddha Hall is among the largest wooden buildings in the world, housing a huge bronze statue of the Buddha Vairocana. Entering through the imposing Nandaimon gate with its guardian statues, you feel the scale of ancient Nara. Outside, the temple sits in Nara Park, where free-roaming deer add to the memorable atmosphere.

What to do

  • See the giant bronze Buddha
  • Pass the Nandaimon guardian gate
  • Admire the huge wooden Great Buddha Hall
  • Meet the deer in Nara Park

Getting there

From Kintetsu-Nara Station it is about a 20-minute walk through Nara Park, or a short bus ride. Nara is roughly 35-45 minutes from Kyoto or about 40 minutes from Osaka-Namba by Kintetsu trains.

When to visit

Spring and autumn are the most pleasant seasons. Arrive early in the morning, before the tour groups and school trips fill the hall.

Photo tips

  • Shoot the Great Buddha Hall from the courtyard to show its scale
  • Frame the Nandaimon gate guardians from below
  • Capture deer with the temple roofs in the background

Nearby

  • Nara Park deer
  • Kasuga Taisha Shrine
  • Nigatsu-do Hall

Good to know

Deer can nibble paper and bags, so keep maps and tickets tucked away and be careful if you buy deer crackers.

Keep voices low in the hall, and do not tease or chase the deer in Nara Park.
