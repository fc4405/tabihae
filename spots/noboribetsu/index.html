Noboribetsu Jigokudani Onsen

Steaming Hell Valley and Hokkaido's most famous onsen town.

  • Best: January, February, June, October, November
  • Onsen towns
  • Scenic nature
  • Time: Half day, or overnight for the full onsen experience

Why go

Noboribetsu Onsen is one of Hokkaido's most famous hot spring resorts, known for its many types of mineral water. Just above the town lies Jigokudani, or Hell Valley, a volcanic crater where steam rises from vents and the hillside glows in tones of yellow, grey and rust. A boardwalk leads through the landscape, and the nearby Oyunuma pond is a hot, steaming pool. Together with demon-themed town decor, it makes a memorable, atmospheric stop.

What to do

  • Walk the boardwalk through steaming Hell Valley
  • See the hot, steaming Oyunuma pond
  • Soak in a hot spring at an onsen inn
  • Spot the demon statues around the town

Getting there

From Sapporo, take the JR limited express to Noboribetsu Station (about 1 hour+), then a bus of around 15 minutes to the onsen town. Jigokudani is a short walk from the town centre.

When to visit

Autumn foliage in October and the snowy winter months are especially atmospheric. Summer is green and pleasant, but the steam looks most dramatic in cold weather.

Photo tips

  • Shoot the steam against the colourful hillside from the boardwalk viewpoints
  • Visit in cold weather for thicker, more dramatic steam clouds
  • Frame the demon statues in the evening glow of the onsen street

Nearby

  • Oyunuma pond, a short trip from the valley
  • Noboribetsu Date Jidaimura, a historical theme park
  • Lake Toya for a scenic lake day trip

Good to know

The valley smells of sulfur, and silver jewelry can tarnish in the onsen, so remove it first. Boardwalks can be slippery in winter, so wear shoes with good grip.

In onsen, wash thoroughly before entering, keep towels out of the water, and no swimwear. Check each facility's tattoo policy in advance.
