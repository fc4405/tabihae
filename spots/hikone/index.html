Shiga · 彦根城

Hikone Castle

A real Edo-era castle above Lake Biwa

  • Best: March, April, October, November
  • Retro Japan
  • Scenic nature
  • Time: 2-3 hours
Plan a November route with this spot

Why go

Hikone Castle sits on a hill beside Lake Biwa in Shiga and was built in the early 17th century for the Ii clan. Its three-story keep is a National Treasure and one of only a dozen castle keeps in Japan that survive from the Edo period or earlier. White walls with graceful gables look especially striking against blue sky or blossoms. The castle grounds, the moat and the nearby Genkyuen garden make for an easy and photogenic half day.

What to do

  • Climb the steep stairs inside the National Treasure keep
  • Walk the castle grounds and moat
  • Stroll Genkyuen garden beside the castle
  • Meet the castle mascot Hikonyan on event days

Getting there

From Kyoto, take the JR Tokaido Main Line (Biwako Line) to Hikone Station, about 1 hour. The castle is roughly a 10-15 minute walk from the station.

When to visit

Late March to April for cherry blossoms, and November for autumn color. Go in the morning for softer light and fewer visitors.

Photo tips

  • Frame the white keep through cherry blossoms in spring
  • Shoot the castle reflected from Genkyuen's pond
  • Photograph the stone walls and gate from the approach path

Nearby

  • Genkyuen garden
  • Yume Kyobashi Castle Road shopping street
  • Omi-Hachiman canal district

Good to know

The keep's staircases are narrow and very steep, so avoid bulky bags and take your time. Carry a small bag for your shoes.

Take off your shoes before entering the keep and carry them in the bag provided. Stay on the marked path and steps.
