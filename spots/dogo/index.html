Dogo Onsen Honkan

Bathe in one of Japan's oldest hot springs.

  • Best: January, March, April, October, November, December
  • Onsen towns
  • Retro Japan
  • Time: 1.5-2.5 hours
Plan a November route with this spot

Why go

Dogo Onsen Honkan is the iconic main bathhouse of Dogo Onsen in Matsuyama, a hot spring with a history said to stretch back some three thousand years. The grand three-storey wooden building dates from the Meiji era and is topped with a white heron figure and a drum tower. It is also tied to Natsume Soseki's novel Botchan, and the surrounding town of arcades and ryokan is wonderfully nostalgic, especially lit up at night.

What to do

  • Admire the heron-topped Meiji wooden bathhouse
  • Soak in the historic public bath
  • Relax with tea and sweets after bathing
  • Stroll the nearby shopping arcades

Getting there

From Matsuyama city centre or JR Matsuyama Station, take the Iyotetsu streetcar to Dogo Onsen, about 25 to 30 minutes; the bathhouse is a short walk away.

When to visit

Evening is the most atmospheric, with the building lit up, but expect queues on weekends. Weekday mornings are calmer.

Photo tips

  • Shoot the facade at dusk when the lights come on.
  • Include the heron on the roof in your frame.
  • Capture the streetcar passing near the station for a nostalgic shot.

Nearby

  • Matsuyama Castle
  • Dogo Park and Yu-jinja shrine
  • Ishiteji temple

Good to know

Check ahead for the latest bathing options and any restoration work, and bring your own towel or rent one on site.

Wash before soaking, keep towels out of the bath and keep your voice low, as locals still use it daily.
