Nagano · 善光寺

Zenko-ji, Nagano

Touch the key to paradise in total darkness beneath the hall.

  • Best: April, May, October, November
  • Retro Japan
  • Scenic nature
  • Time: 1.5-2 hours
Plan a November route with this spotTours & tickets nearby (Klook)

Why go

Zenko-ji is one of Japan's most beloved temples and has welcomed pilgrims for well over a thousand years. It is known for a hidden principal Buddha image that is never shown to the public. Visitors approach along a lively stone-paved street lined with shops, passing the great Sanmon gate before reaching the huge main hall. Beneath the hall, a pitch-dark passage lets you feel your way to touch a lock said to connect you to paradise.

What to do

  • Pass through the grand Sanmon gate
  • Visit the vast main hall
  • Walk the dark passage beneath the hall
  • Browse shops along the approach street

Getting there

From Nagano Station, take a bus toward the temple for about 10 minutes, or walk for roughly 30 minutes up the gentle approach. Nagano is reached from Tokyo by Hokuriku Shinkansen in around 1.5 hours.

When to visit

Early morning is calm and atmospheric, and autumn colors make it especially pretty. Weekdays are quieter than weekends and holidays.

Photo tips

  • Frame the Sanmon gate from the stone-paved approach
  • Shoot the main hall's grand roof from the courtyard
  • Capture incense smoke and worshippers for atmosphere

Nearby

  • Nakamise approach street
  • Nagano Station area cafes
  • Old townhouse streets near the temple

Good to know

Wear shoes that are easy to take off and carry a small bag, as you may be asked to remove shoes for the passage beneath the hall.

Bow at the gate, keep quiet in the main hall, and do not take photos inside the hall.
