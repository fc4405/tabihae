Fukuoka · 太宰府天満宮

Dazaifu Tenmangu

Pray for exam luck under plum blossoms

  • Best: February, March, November
  • Retro Japan
  • Photogenic cafes
  • Time: 2-3 hours
Plan a November route with this spot

Why go

Dazaifu Tenmangu is a Shinto shrine dedicated to Sugawara no Michizane, a Heian-period scholar revered as the god of learning. Students from all over Japan come to pray for exam success. Graceful arched bridges cross a heart-shaped pond, and thousands of plum trees bloom here in late winter. The lively approach street is lined with shops selling umegae mochi, a grilled rice cake, and one branch of a well-known coffee chain here is a striking modern wooden building.

What to do

  • Cross the arched bridges over the pond
  • See the plum trees bloom in late winter
  • Taste umegae mochi along the approach street
  • Visit the main hall and write a wish on an ema plaque

Getting there

From Fukuoka's Tenjin area, take the Nishitetsu line to Nishitetsu Futsukaichi and change to the Dazaifu line, about 30-40 minutes. The shrine is a short walk from Dazaifu Station.

When to visit

Late February to early March for plum blossoms and November for autumn leaves. Weekday mornings are calmer, especially before exam season.

Photo tips

  • Shoot the arched bridge with the pond and trees
  • Capture plum blossoms with the main hall behind
  • Photograph the shop-lined approach street in the morning

Nearby

  • Kyushu National Museum
  • Komyozenji temple and its moss and rock garden
  • Tenjin shopping district in Fukuoka

Good to know

Try umegae mochi fresh and warm, and expect heavy crowds on weekends and around New Year and exam season.

Bow at the torii, rinse your hands at the purification fountain, and bow twice, clap twice, then bow once when praying.
