Tsukiji Outer Market

Breakfast in Tokyo's most famous food lanes.

  • Best: March, April, October, November, December
  • Photogenic cafes
  • Modern & urban
  • Time: 1.5-2 hours
Plan a November route with this spotTours & tickets nearby (Klook)

Why go

Tsukiji Outer Market is the maze of small shops and food stalls that grew up beside Tokyo's old wholesale fish market. The inner market moved to Toyosu in 2018, but the outer market stayed and is still lined with seafood vendors, knife shops and tiny eateries. It is a favourite for a morning of grilled seafood, sweet tamagoyaki omelette and sushi counters, with endless colourful food shots for your feed.

What to do

  • Sweet tamagoyaki rolled fresh at stalls
  • Sushi and kaisen-don seafood bowls for breakfast
  • Grilled scallops and other skewered seafood
  • Japanese knives and kitchenware shops

Getting there

From central Tokyo take the Hibiya Line to Tsukiji Station, or the Oedo Line to Tsukijishijo Station; both are a short walk from the market.

When to visit

Go on a weekday morning to beat the crowds, since many stalls wind down by early afternoon. Weekends are lively but very packed.

Photo tips

  • Shoot the colourful seafood displays from slightly above for the best colours.
  • Ask the vendor before photographing a stall up close.
  • Try the narrow side lanes for atmospheric shots with fewer crowds.

Nearby

  • Tsukiji Hongan-ji temple
  • Hamarikyu Gardens
  • Ginza shopping district

Good to know

Bring some cash, as many small stalls do not accept cards, and avoid big backpacks and suitcases in the crowded lanes.

The lanes are narrow and crowded, so finish your food at the stall instead of eating while walking.
