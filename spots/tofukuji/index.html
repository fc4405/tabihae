Kyoto · 東福寺

Tofuku-ji Autumn Leaves

Kyoto's most famous sea of crimson maple leaves.

  • Best: May, November
  • Retro Japan
  • Scenic nature
  • Time: 1.5-2 hours
Plan a November route with this spotTours & tickets nearby (Klook)

Why go

Tofuku-ji is a large Rinzai Zen temple in southern Kyoto, founded in the 13th century. Its Tsutenkyo covered bridge crosses a valley filled with maple trees that turn brilliant red in late autumn. The temple also has an imposing wooden main gate and a modern checkerboard-style garden at the abbot's quarters. Visiting in autumn is unforgettable, while the same valley glows fresh green in early summer.

What to do

  • Look across the maple valley from Tsutenkyo bridge
  • See the imposing wooden Sanmon main gate
  • Admire the modern checkerboard garden at the Hojo
  • Enjoy fresh green maples in early summer

Getting there

From Kyoto Station, ride the JR Nara Line one stop to Tofukuji Station, then walk about 10 minutes. The Keihan Line's Tofukuji Station is also nearby.

When to visit

Mid to late November is peak foliage; go right at opening on a weekday morning to avoid the worst crowds. Early summer is calm and green.

Photo tips

  • Shoot from the bridge or the valley floor to frame the red leaves with the wooden structure
  • Look for backlit maple leaves to make the colours glow
  • Use the quieter garden walls and gravel for minimalist compositions

Nearby

  • Fushimi Inari Taisha, one JR stop away
  • Sanjusangendo Hall with its rows of Kannon statues
  • Sennyu-ji, an imperial temple nearby

Good to know

In peak foliage the bridge area gets very crowded and can have queues, so arrive early and carry some cash for admission.

Keep voices low in temple halls, remove shoes where required, and follow signs about where photography is not allowed.
