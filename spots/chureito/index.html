Fujiyoshida · 忠霊塔

Chureito Pagoda

Fuji, a red pagoda and cherry blossoms in one postcard frame

  • Best: April, October, November
  • Scenic nature
  • Time: 1-1.5 hours
Plan a November route with this spotTours & tickets nearby (Klook)

Why go

Chureito Pagoda is a vermilion five-storey pagoda on the hillside of Arakurayama Sengen Park in Fujiyoshida. It was built in 1963 as a peace memorial for the war dead, which is what 'Chureito' (loyal spirit tower) refers to. From the small deck beside it, the pagoda, the town below and Mt Fuji line up so neatly that the view became one of Japan's most recognisable images. It is at its most striking with spring cherry blossoms or autumn maples, and even a clear winter morning with a snowy Fuji feels magical.

What to do

  • The classic Fuji, pagoda and town view from the observation deck
  • The long stone stairway up through the park (roughly 400 steps)
  • Cherry blossoms in spring and red maples in autumn framing the pagoda
  • The quiet shrine and memorial area beside the pagoda

Getting there

From Tokyo take the JR Chuo Line to Otsuki, then the Fujikyuko Line to Shimoyoshida Station (about 2.5 hours in total); the park entrance is a 10-15 minute walk from the station. Direct highway buses to Kawaguchiko also pass nearby.

When to visit

Mid-April for cherry blossoms and early-to-mid November for autumn colour are the peaks; Fuji is clearest on cold, dry mornings. Go on a weekday morning, since the deck is tiny and packed in blossom season.

Photo tips

  • Shoot from the main deck with a 50-100mm lens to stack Fuji, pagoda and town together
  • Arrive early in the morning when clouds are least likely to hide Fuji
  • Include the foreground blossoms or maple branches to add depth and colour

Nearby

  • Lake Kawaguchiko lakeside views of Fuji
  • Oshino Hakkai spring ponds
  • Fujiyoshida Honcho-dori street with Fuji at its end

Good to know

It is a steep climb of about 400 steps, so wear comfortable shoes and allow breathing room. Fuji is often hidden by cloud, so check the weather and visibility before you go. Stay on the paths and be quiet near the memorial area.
