Hida-Takayama Old Town

Step back into the Edo period in the Japanese Alps.

  • Best: February, April, August, October, November
  • Retro Japan
  • Photogenic cafes
  • Time: 2-3 hours (or a full day with the museums)
Plan a November route with this spotTours & tickets nearby (Klook)

Why go

Takayama's old town, often called Sanmachi, is a preserved district of dark wooden merchant houses with latticed fronts that date back to the Edo period. Wealthy from timber and carpentry, the town built these streets beside narrow canals, and several sake breweries still hang cedar balls above their doors. It is a calm, photogenic place to stroll, sample local sweets and Hida beef, and soak in the feel of old Japan.

What to do

  • Stroll the Sanmachi Suji merchant streets
  • See cedar balls hanging at sake breweries
  • Visit a morning market by the river
  • Taste Hida beef skewers and local sweets

Getting there

From Nagoya take the JR Limited Express Hida to Takayama Station, about 2.5 to 3 hours, then walk roughly 10 minutes to the old town.

When to visit

Visit in the morning for quiet streets and the morning markets. Spring and autumn festivals and winter snow are especially atmospheric.

Photo tips

  • Shoot down the street early in the morning to avoid crowds in the frame.
  • Frame the cedar balls and wooden lattices as close-up details.
  • Come back at dusk when the lanterns and wooden facades glow warmly.

Nearby

  • Takayama Jinya historic government house
  • Miyagawa Morning Market
  • Hida Folk Village

Good to know

Many shops close in the early evening, so plan your sightseeing and snacks for the daytime.

Many old houses are private homes and working shops, so do not enter without invitation and keep your voice down.
