Kiyosumi-shirakawa

Tokyo's quiet coffee district of warehouses, roasters and gardens.

  • Best: All year
  • Photogenic cafes
  • Modern & urban
  • Time: 2-3 hours
Plan a November route with this spotTours & tickets nearby (Klook)

Why go

Kiyosumi-shirakawa lies east of the Sumida River in Koto Ward, an old canal and warehouse district shaped by the Edo-period timber trade. In recent years the spacious warehouses have been turned into independent coffee roasters and cafes, and Blue Bottle opened its first Japan location here. The neighbourhood is slower and quieter than central Tokyo, with the old Kiyosumi Garden and the Museum of Contemporary Art Tokyo nearby. It is a good place to spend a relaxed afternoon moving from cup to cup.

What to do

  • Specialty coffee at independent roasters and warehouse cafes
  • Blue Bottle's first Japan cafe in a converted warehouse
  • Strolling the historic Kiyosumi Garden with its pond and stones
  • Museum of Contemporary Art Tokyo, a short walk away

Getting there

Kiyosumi-shirakawa Station is served by the Tokyo Metro Hanzomon Line and the Toei Oedo Line. From Shibuya or Otemachi, the Hanzomon Line takes you there without changing.

When to visit

Go on a weekday for shorter queues at the popular cafes, since weekends get busy. Late morning to mid-afternoon is ideal, and many shops close on certain weekdays, so check ahead.

Photo tips

  • Photograph the cafe interiors in soft daylight, using the high warehouse ceilings and big windows
  • Capture latte art and pour-over steam from a low angle on the counter
  • In Kiyosumi Garden, frame the pond, stepping stones and old pines for a classic Japanese garden shot

Nearby

  • Kiyosumi Garden, a Meiji-era strolling garden with a central pond
  • Museum of Contemporary Art Tokyo, a large museum of postwar and contemporary art
  • Fukagawa Edo Museum, a life-size recreation of an Edo-period neighbourhood

Good to know

Cafes here are small and many do not take large groups or long stays at peak hours, so go in pairs and be ready to wait. Check the roasters' opening days, and carry cash as a backup.
