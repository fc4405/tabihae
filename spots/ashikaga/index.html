Tochigi · あしかがフラワーパーク

Ashikaga Flower Park

Walk under a living ceiling of purple wisteria blossoms.

  • Best: April, May, November, December
  • Scenic nature
  • Time: 2-3 hours
Plan a November route with this spotTours & tickets nearby (Klook)

Why go

Ashikaga Flower Park in Tochigi is famed for its wisteria, with huge old vines trained into sprawling canopies and long flower tunnels. The season usually peaks from late April to early May, when the air fills with a sweet scent and the colours range from deep purple to white and pink. In the evening, illuminations make the blossoms glow, and the park also holds a popular winter light display. It is one of Japan's most photogenic spring flower spots.

What to do

  • Stroll beneath the huge wisteria trellises
  • Walk through the long wisteria flower tunnels
  • See the illuminated blossoms after dusk
  • Visit the winter light display season

Getting there

From Tokyo, take a train toward Oyama and change to the JR Ryomo Line to Ashikaga Flower Park Station, about 2 hours in total. In peak season, expect queues and extra services.

When to visit

Late April to early May is the wisteria season; arrive at opening on a weekday, and stay into the evening to see the illuminations. Check the bloom forecast before you go.

Photo tips

  • Stand at the tunnel entrance and shoot down its length for strong leading lines
  • Look up and shoot against the sky to capture the cascading flowers
  • Try dusk when blossoms and lights balance for the best colour

Nearby

  • Ashikaga Gakko, one of Japan's oldest schools, now a historic site
  • Banna-ji, a historic temple linked to the Ashikaga clan
  • Sano, nearby, famed for its ramen

Good to know

Peak weekends are very crowded and queues for entry can be long, so book tickets in advance if possible and go on a weekday.

Stay on the paths, never pull or touch the blossoms, and keep tripods and selfie sticks out of the crowded walkways.
