Kinosaki Onsen

Yukata strolls and seven bathhouses in a riverside onsen town.

  • Best: January, February, March, April, November, December
  • Onsen towns
  • Retro Japan
  • Time: Overnight stay recommended (day trip: 4-6 hours)

Why go

Kinosaki Onsen is a hot spring town in northern Hyogo with a long history as a healing retreat. Its charm lies in the custom of staying at a ryokan, putting on yukata and wooden geta, and walking to seven public bathhouses along a willow-lined canal. The town is compact and especially romantic in the evening, with lanterns, steam and the sound of geta on the stone paths. Winter also brings the local crab season.

What to do

  • Hop between the seven public bathhouses
  • Stroll in yukata and geta along the willows
  • Ride the ropeway up to Onsenji temple
  • Try local seafood and crab in winter

Getting there

From Kyoto or Osaka take the JR Limited Express Kounotori to Kinosaki-Onsen Station, roughly 2.5 to 3 hours, then walk or take a short taxi to the town.

When to visit

Winter is the favourite season for crab and cosy bathing, while spring blossoms along the river are also lovely. Evenings are the most atmospheric.

Photo tips

  • Shoot the willow-lined canal at dusk when the lanterns come on.
  • Capture geta and yukata details on the stone bridges.
  • Photograph the bathhouse exteriors only, never inside.

Nearby

  • Kinosaki Onsen Ropeway and Onsenji temple
  • Kinosaki Marine World
  • Genbudo Cave

Good to know

Most ryokan lend yukata and offer a pass for the public baths, and tattoo rules vary by bathhouse, so check in advance.

Wash thoroughly before entering a bath, keep towels out of the water and do not take photos inside bathhouses.
