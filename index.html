    Tax-free shopping is changing (from Nov 2026)

    Japan is switching to a "refund" system: you pay full price in-store, then get the tax refunded at the airport before check-in, after a passport scan at a tax-free terminal (or in advance via Visit Japan Web at major airports). Keep your receipts and purchases accessible — not in checked luggage — until that's done.

    Register in Visit Japan Web →

    If you leave something behind

    Japan's lost-and-found culture genuinely works in your favor. On a train: go to the station where you boarded or got off and ask staff, or call that railway's own lost-property line. In a taxi: your receipt has the company's phone number — call them directly. In a shop: go back and ask; if nothing turns up, the nearest police station (kōban) is the next step.
